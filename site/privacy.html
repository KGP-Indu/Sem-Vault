Sem Vault IM, IIT Kharagpur

Privacy

Sem Vault is a free library of study material run by IM students at IIT Kharagpur. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. This page says what it stores and why.

Browsing

You can browse and download without signing in. The site keeps a copy of the file list in your browser for a few minutes so pages load faster. Nothing about you is sent to us.

Uploading

When you upload, we store the file, its LaTeX source if you add one, the details you type (course, title, description) and, only if you fill them in, your name and email. Your name and email are seen only by the mods who review uploads. Once a mod approves an upload, the file and its details are public on this site. Rejected files are kept in a private folder.

Professor tips

When you send a tip, we store the professor's name, the course, the year, your tip and, only if you fill it in, your name. A mod reads every tip first. Approved tips are public on the professor's page, with your name if you gave one. Tips are about teaching, grading and exams; personal remarks are not published.

Signing in (mods only)

Mods sign in with Google. We receive your name and email address from Google and use the email only to check whether you are on the mod list. We do not get your password or access to anything else in your Google account. When a mod approves, rejects or edits something, their email is saved in a log that only mods can see.

Where it is stored

Files and records live in a Google Drive folder and a Google Sheet owned by the Sem Vault account, kgp.indu@gmail.com. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Google and GitHub have their own privacy policies.

Removing something

To have a file you uploaded, or your name or email, removed, write to kgp.indu@gmail.com. The same address is for any other question about this page.
